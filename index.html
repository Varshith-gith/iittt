Welcome to the Geo-Intel Lab

Harnessing Spatial Intelligence for Viksit Bharat

The Geo-Intel Lab stands at the forefront of India's geospatial revolution—powering data-driven solutions for governance, innovation, and societal impact. Established under the Technology Innovation Hub (TIH) at IIT Tirupati, the lab serves as a national platform for advancing Geospatial Intelligence, Remote Sensing, GNSS, PNT, Digital Twins, and AI-powered decision systems.

In alignment with the vision of Viksit Bharat @2047, the Geo-Intel Lab is committed to building a future-ready India—where spatial thinking enables inclusive development, precision technologies improve livelihoods, and open geospatial ecosystems accelerate public service delivery.

Vision

To become India's premier center for geospatial and PNT innovation, enabling a Viksit Bharat through the development of intelligent applications and platforms that serve public good and transform governance.

Mission

  • Advance interdisciplinary R&D in geospatial analytics, GNSS, PNT, remote sensing, and spatial AI
  • Design scalable applications and digital platforms for ministries, urban local bodies, and public service delivery
  • Build federated national repositories and open data ecosystems
  • Develop indigenous solutions in positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT)
  • Enable Digital Twin platforms and decision dashboards for infrastructure, climate, and disaster management
  • Drive capacity building via training programs, academic networks, and national challenges
  • Promote open innovation by connecting academia, startups, government, and industry
  • Support India's Digital Governance mission through impact-oriented tools aligned with the National Geospatial Policy

Our lab is not just about research—it is about building real-world solutions that serve people, systems, and the nation.

Driving Public Good through Spatial Innovation

Rooted in the principles of public good, the lab enables:

  • Real-time spatial monitoring for disaster management, urban resilience, and climate adaptation
  • Open data platforms that empower citizens, researchers, and policymakers alike
  • Skilling and capacity-building through programs like VidyaGIS and Train-the-Trainers
  • Startup incubation and research translation for impact at scale

Whether it's enhancing crop forecasting, planning smart cities, or tracking carbon emissions, our tools and platforms are designed to create measurable outcomes for people and the planet.

A National Hub for Digital Governance

As India accelerates its Digital Governance mission, the Geo-Intel Lab supports:

  • Geo-enabled decision-making systems for ministries, line departments, and local governments
  • Federated data infrastructures aligned with the National Geospatial Policy 2022
  • Digital Twin technologies to simulate, analyze, and optimize infrastructure and environment
  • Geo-AI frameworks for predictive analytics, satellite imagery interpretation, and anomaly detection

Through our partnerships with academia, industry, startups and government, we are transforming how the nation visualizes and solves its most pressing challenges.
